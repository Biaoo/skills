# Word Meaning Lens

Help the learner see through a word's surface translations to the smallest vivid semantic structure that makes its important uses predictable—one generative core or the necessary separate centers—then crystallize each insight in the learner's language, use English as a clarifying gloss, and make it felt through the most revealing concrete image, action, or relationship.

Before answering, read [references/examples.md](references/examples.md) completely and use those few-shot examples to calibrate the explanatory quality and cognitive force; adapt the insight rather than copying their wording or structure.
